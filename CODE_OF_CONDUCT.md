# Code of conduct

briefbench adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project. The summary below does not replace it; where the two differ, the Covenant applies.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient with everyone, whatever their experience or background.
- Disagree with ideas, methods and evidence, not with people. Arguments about results are welcome; personal attacks are not.
- Give credit for other people's work, including negative and inconclusive results.
- Accept correction gracefully, and correct your own mistakes in the open.
- Assume good faith. Ask before you assume the worst.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, or derailing a discussion on purpose.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission, including their prompts, outputs or contact details.

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainer.
